# Employee Salary Management System
**Incubyte Engineering Assessment | Requirements & Scope | Version 0.1**

## 1. Goal and Problem Statement

ACME is an organization with 10,000 employees across multiple countries. Currently, the HR team manages employee salary information through Excel spreadsheets, making data maintenance and salary analysis tedious.

The goal is to build a web-based Employee Salary Management System that allows HR managers to manage employee salary records efficiently and answer questions about how the organization compensates its employees.

## 2. Target User

**HR Manager:** Responsible for maintaining employee salary information, reviewing compensation data, and understanding salary distribution across the organization.

## 3. Proposed Functional Requirements

- **Employee Management:** View, add, update, search, and filter employee records.
- **Salary Management:** View and update employee salaries with appropriate input validation.
- **HR Dashboard:** Display total employees, salary summaries, and payroll information.
- **Salary Analytics:** Display organization-wide salary totals and average salary, with employee-level salary search and sorting.
- **Search and Pagination:** Efficiently navigate records for 10,000 employees.
- **Data Seeding:** Provide a script to generate 10,000 realistic sample employee records.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Performance:** Efficient database queries, server-side pagination, and responsive UI.
- **Maintainability:** Clear separation of responsibilities, readable code, and modular architecture.
- **Reliability:** Meaningful unit tests covering core business logic and validation.
- **Data Integrity:** Prevent invalid salary values and maintain consistent employee records.
- **Security:** Validate inputs and restrict sensitive salary operations as appropriate to the agreed scope.

## 5. Proposed Technology Stack

- **Backend:** Java 21, Spring Boot, REST APIs
- **Frontend:** Angular, TypeScript
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Testing:** JUnit 5, Mockito, and frontend tests where appropriate
- **Version Control:** Git with incremental, meaningful commits
- **Deployment:** Publicly accessible demonstration environment

## 6. Initial Scope Exclusions

To keep the solution focused, the following are excluded unless clarified as required:

- Payroll processing and tax calculations
- Bank transfers and salary disbursements
- Integration with external HR platforms
- Complex approval workflows
- Advanced predictive analytics
- Country-specific compensation analytics and currency conversion
- Authentication and role-based access control (prototype only; required before production use)

## 7. Open Questions

1. Should the system support multiple currencies and currency conversion?
2. Is historical salary tracking required?
3. Is authentication or role-based access control expected?
4. Should compensation include bonuses and allowances, or only base salary?

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

- HR managers can view and manage employee and salary information through a functional web UI.
- The system supports a seeded dataset of 10,000 employees.
- Search, filtering, and pagination work correctly.
- Salary summaries and analytics provide meaningful information.
- Core business functionality is covered by deterministic automated tests.
- The application is deployed and demonstrated through a video.
- The Git repository includes documentation, architectural decisions, AI usage notes, and incremental commits.

**Design Principle:** Prioritize simplicity, correctness, maintainability, and practical product value over unnecessary architectural complexity.
